How to Play

"How Well Do You Know Me?" is a pass-and-play party game for 2 players. Test how well you really know your friend, partner, sibling, or parent!

Quick Play Mode

  1. Choose your relationship type (Friend, Partner, Sibling, or Parent)
  2. Enter both players' names
  3. Select "Quick Play" — the game uses 100 pre-made questions
  4. Pass the phone — Player 2 guesses answers about Player 1
  5. After 10 questions, swap roles — Player 1 now guesses about Player 2
  6. Keep the heart alive! Wrong answers crack it, correct answers heal it
  7. Game ends when the heart shatters — highest score wins!

Personal Mode

  1. Choose your relationship type and enter names
  2. Select "Personal Mode" — hand the phone to the question setter
  3. The setter sees 100 pre-written questions and fills in their own 5 personal answers for each
  4. Mark which answer is TRUE, then save — minimum 10 questions to start
  5. Start the game and pass the phone to the guesser!

Game Mechanics

❤️ The Heart

A shared heart sits on a hill with 10 HP. Wrong answers crack pieces off with visible debris. Correct answers heal it with a green glow. When HP hits 0, the heart explodes — game over!

⏱️ Timer

Each question has a 30-second timer. If time runs out, it counts as a wrong answer.

🔥 Streaks

Get 2+ correct answers in a row for a streak bonus indicator.

🔄 Pass & Play

After each round of 10 questions, players swap roles. A "Pass the Phone" screen with a countdown ensures no peeking!

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app require an internet connection?

An internet connection is needed only on first launch to load fonts. After that, the game works completely offline.

Is my data stored or shared?

No. All game data (names, answers, scores) exists only in temporary memory during gameplay and is discarded when the app closes. Nothing is stored, transmitted, or shared. See our Privacy Policy.

What ages is this game appropriate for?

The game is suitable for ages 4+. It contains no violence, profanity, or objectionable content. Questions are lighthearted and focused on getting to know each other better.

Can I play with more than 2 people?

The current version supports 2 players in pass-and-play mode. Group play features may be added in future updates!
