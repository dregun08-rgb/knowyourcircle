Privacy Policy

How Well Do You Know Me? by WOW Studios

Effective Date: June 6, 2026

Overview

"How Well Do You Know Me?" is a social party game designed to be played in person with friends, family, partners, and siblings. The app operates entirely offline on your device. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any user data whatsoever. Specifically:

  • No account creation or login is required
  • No personal data (names, emails, phone numbers) is collected or stored
  • No gameplay data or scores are transmitted off your device
  • No analytics, tracking, or usage data is collected
  • No cookies or local storage is used to persist data between sessions
  • No location data is accessed
  • No camera, microphone, contacts, or other device permissions are requested

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or data collection services. The only external network requests are loading open-source JavaScript libraries (React) and web fonts (Google Fonts) from content delivery networks at app startup. No user data is sent to these services.

Data Storage

All game data (player names, questions, answers, scores) exists only in temporary device memory during an active game session. When the app is closed, all data is immediately discarded. Nothing is written to disk or transmitted over the network.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app is suitable for all ages and contains no objectionable content.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
